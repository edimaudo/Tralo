Governance Framework

Tralo provides an institutional-grade oversight layer for managing reporting compliance across credit portfolios.

Monitoring Logic

The system calculates Total Critical Exposure by identifying all facilities where a mandatory reporting deadline has passed without document submission. This figure represents the total capital currently in a state of technical breach.

  • Proactive Alerting: Risk levels shift to "High" when a deadline is within 48 hours.
  • Workflow Escalation: Breaches are routed to Credit Risk Officers (CRO), while upcoming tasks remain with Relationship Managers (RM).

Operational Glossary

Facility Provision:Specific financial or administrative terms within the credit agreement.
Reporting Window:The timeframe allotted for a borrower to submit mandatory financial data.
Workflow Action:Professional steps required to remediate current risk levels.
