Overview

Operational Compliance Bridge

Tralo bridges the critical gap between static loan agreements and operational compliance. It transforms legal text into a live, reactive monitoring environment.

The Problem

Information delivery and financial covenants are often buried in PDFs, leading to "Silent Defaults" and manual oversight errors.

The Solution

A digitized monitoring system that ensures obligations are surfaced, validated, and remediated in real-time.

App Design

The Compliance Engine

Risk Analysis

Automated prioritization based on "Time-to-Default", ensuring urgent obligations surface to the top of the workflow.

Stakeholder Routing

Direct escalation paths based on risk severity, automating the internal remediation chain from RM to CRO.

Exposure Valuation

Real-time calculation of Capital at Risk, providing an immediate financial view of portfolio vulnerability.

Governance Logic

  • • Temporal Awareness: System scans all milestones relative to today's date.
  • • Dynamic Severity: Breach risk increases exponentially as "Time-to-Default" narrows.
  • • Portfolio Health: Aggregated stability scoring across all business units.
